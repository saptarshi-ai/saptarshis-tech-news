# Privacy policy

Last updated: 16/10/2026

This app (n8n - Saptarshi Tech Hub) is a personal automation used only by its owner, Saptarshi Sengupta. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google Sheets access: the app reads and updates the owner's own post queue spreadsheet.

Gmail access: the app sends post drafts to the owner's own inbox for approval.

How data is used: only to draft, approve and publish the owner's LinkedIn posts. The app runs on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The only data sent outside Google is post text sent to Google Gemini to draft the post.

Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
